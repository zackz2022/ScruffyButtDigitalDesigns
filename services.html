04 · Design ownership & licensing

You purchase the design. You receive the agreed rights.

Eligible commissioned artwork may include transfer of the legally transferable copyright rights identified in a written agreement. Ownership transfers only after the project is completed, full payment is received, and all required documents are signed.

Potential Customer Rights

Personal or business use, reproduction, printing on merchandise, branding, modification, and authorized commercial use may be included to the extent expressly agreed.

Important Limitations

Third-party images, fonts, trademarks, licensed materials, and AI-generated elements may carry separate restrictions. Artwork ownership does not guarantee trademark availability or exclusive rights to unprotectable elements.

Completed original artwork will not be resold or licensed to another customer after a valid copyright transfer, subject to applicable exceptions and rights the creator does not own.
